# Demo material

**Audio:** *Electrician to Astronaut*, Houston We Have a Podcast, episode 327 — NASA
Johnson Space Center. Downloaded from NASA's official podcast feed.

NASA material is generally not protected by copyright: works produced by the
US federal government are in the public domain. See
<https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/>.

Re-encoded here to 64 kbps mono AAC (`episode.m4a`, 18 MB) so the whole episode
caches in the browser and every seek is instant.

**Transcript:** generated locally with whisper.cpp (`ggml-medium-q5_0`) at token
level, then regrouped into lines. Not an official NASA transcript — expect the
occasional mis-heard proper noun.
